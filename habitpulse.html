A few useful answers.

No manual required; just the bits people ask.

How do streaks work?

A day counts only when the full target is met on a scheduled day. Unscheduled days and explicit pause periods are skipped; a missed planned day breaks the current run, but never erases your history.

How does scheduling work?

Daily, selected weekdays and repeating intervals are supported. A new habit starts counting on its creation date. Change or pause the schedule whenever it stops fitting.

Where is my data?

Habits, check-ins, goals and notes stay in this browser by default. Export a JSON backup before moving browsers or devices. Cloud sync is not connected in this copy.

How do I export or delete data?

Open Privacy & terms from the footer. JSON keeps the full record; CSV is handy for a spreadsheet. Delete local data removes the browser copy after confirmation.

Are reminders or integrations active?

No. This copy does not send notifications or connect calendars, health services, wearables or social accounts. It will not claim a connection that is not there.

What does Pro unlock?

Nothing is being sold or paywalled here yet. Contact support if you want to ask about a future plan; no subscription starts from this page.
