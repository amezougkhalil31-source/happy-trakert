Privacy

HabitPulse stores habits, schedules, check-ins and reflections in local browser storage by default. This local-only data is not sent to HabitPulse servers. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to view it; do not enter sensitive health, financial or other confidential information.

If an operator configures Supabase and you choose to create an account, account details and any future sync data are handled by that project under its configured privacy and retention rules. This copy does not currently synchronize local habits to Supabase. Optional daily email nudges are off unless you explicitly opt in. Email delivery requires a separately configured provider.

Contact and Pro links do nothing until you choose them. WhatsApp opens a message draft addressed to +212 660 725 091. Email opens a draft addressed to Amezougkhalil31@gmail.com. Your device, browser and those providers apply their own privacy policies.

You can remove local data using your browser's site-data controls. There is no analytics or advertising code in this app.

Terms

HabitPulse is a personal organization tool, not medical, mental-health, financial or professional advice. Use it at your own pace. Missed check-ins do not create penalties, and statistics are estimates based on the schedule and data you enter.

The app is provided as-is, without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability or data recovery. You are responsible for keeping exports or backups of information you need. Do not use the app for emergencies or time-critical reminders.

There is no paid subscription or Pro entitlement enabled in this local copy. Any future paid feature must show its price, renewal terms, cancellation method and applicable consumer terms before purchase.
